### Changes

- Professional original line-art SUV and sedan perspective drawings instead of crude geometric car.
- Drawings are embedded in the client SVG for offline-compatible print/PDF capture.
- Transparent clickable panel zones over the drawing, with severity colour highlights.
- Existing Left/Right/Front/Rear/Top orthographic vector modes, saved job assessments, photo packs, 3D assets and PDF output preserved.
- SUV / wagon uses SUV detailed drawing, sedan / coupe uses sedan drawing. Hatchback, bakkie and van continue using generic line-based orthographic illustrations; a manufacturer-exact model isn't claimed.

### Important
The drawings represent vehicle **body type**, not a specific Toyota RAV4. Colour appears as the body-colour chip and coloured damage markings; the technical line art stays black and white. Panel hotspots are approximate and may not precisely follow body-panel seams. Please inspect and select the labelled panel before saving.
