SULMOS26 PANEL BEATER — V40 PROFESSIONAL DOCUMENT PDF PATCH
=============================================================

WHAT THIS CHANGES
- Downloaded repair estimates, customer invoices and workshop paperwork have a new A4 visual layout.
- Uploaded company logos are scaled into a neat company header.
- When a company has no logo, the document shows a neutral "NO LOGO UPLOADED" placeholder.
- The repair assessment and item table wrap onto additional pages rather than getting cut off.
- Subtotal, stored VAT and total appear immediately after the item table.
- Footer terms and the source company identity appear on each document.

FILE TO REPLACE
    public/app.js

INSTALLATION
1. Open https://github.com/mos00000006/sulmos26-panel-beaterss
2. Back up the existing public/app.js (download it or use Git history).
3. Open public/app.js and choose the pencil/edit button.
4. Replace its entire content with the app.js from this patch (or upload app.js into the public folder).
5. Commit to the main branch.
6. Run the existing "Panel Beater Check" workflow.
7. Run the existing "Deploy SULMOS26 Panel Beater V38" workflow.
8. Open a saved estimate and click Download PDF. Check the downloaded file.
9. Upload a company logo under Company Branding; save; download again to check your real company logo.

IMPORTANT
- DO NOT overwrite src/index.js, migration files, Cloudflare secrets, or your Retail repository.
- This is a front-end PDF template change only; no existing invoices or estimates are edited.
- VAT values are sourced from saved records; the patch does not certify SARS VAT tax invoice compliance.
- Sample PDF contains fictional demonstration data, not a real company invoice.

LOCAL CHECKS
- node --check public/app.js
- Chromium rendering of one-page estimate and six-page extended estimate
- Generated PDF opened and inspected with pdfinfo
